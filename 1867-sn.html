About This Map

This map is titled Town of Norwalk, Fairfield Co., Conn., and comes from an atlas published in 1867 by Frederick W. Beers. I think it's from An Atlas of New York and Vicinity.

This map focuses on the Borough of Norwalk and South Norwalk, or in other words the built-up areas. There's another map from the same atlas that covers the outlying areas of Norwalk more broadly.

Source: David Rumsey Map Collection
