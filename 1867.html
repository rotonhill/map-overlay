About This Map

This map is titled Town of Norwalk, Fairfield Co., Conn., and comes from an atlas published in 1867 by Frederick W. Beers. I think it's from Atlas of New York and Vicinity.

This map has more detail of the countryside of Norwalk, outside of the towns, including Roton Hill. There's another map from the same atlas that focuses on the in-town areas. The source map also includes a section that focuses on Winnipauk.

Source: David Rumsey Map Collection
